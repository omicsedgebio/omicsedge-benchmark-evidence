# Third-Party Data and Benchmark Materials

The MIT License in this repository applies to original OmicsEdgeBio software
code unless otherwise stated.

This repository also contains or references benchmark inputs, metadata,
derived evidence, identifiers, truth resources, stratifications, comparator
outputs, and other materials originating from third-party public resources.

These include resources associated with projects and organizations such as:

- Genome in a Bottle (GIAB)
- NIST
- precisionFDA Truth Challenge V2
- public sequencing submissions used by the benchmark protocols

Third-party materials remain subject to the licenses, terms, attribution
requirements, and usage conditions of their respective sources.

The presence of a third-party resource, identifier, derived record, checksum,
or provenance reference in this repository does not transfer ownership of the
underlying source material to OmicsEdgeBio and does not replace the original
source terms.

Users are responsible for reviewing the applicable source terms before
redistributing third-party data or derived artifacts.

The OmicsEdge Benchmark Evidence Registry is a research resource. It does not
provide clinical interpretation, pathogenicity assessment, technology
ranking, or medical advice.
